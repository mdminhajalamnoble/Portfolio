About

Noble works where hospitality operations meet AI‑driven creative production — coordinating people and deadlines by day, producing content by night.

Currently completing a Bachelor's in Tourism & Hospitality Management at World University of Bangladesh, Noble has built a working profile across two disciplines that rarely sit together: the structured, guest‑facing world of event coordination, and the fast‑moving, tool‑heavy world of AI content production.

That combination plays out in practice. He leads volunteer teams through the logistics of large motorsport events with the URE Race Crew, then applies the same instinct for deadlines and audience awareness to producing short‑form AI‑generated video for Lovora, a live relationship and couple‑activities app.

Photography and cinematography sit underneath both — years of client‑facing shoot work, first with The Photo Story and now as a freelancer, have sharpened the habit that carries through everything he does: capture the right moment, deliver on time, keep things organized.
